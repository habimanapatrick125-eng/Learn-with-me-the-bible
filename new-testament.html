What is the New Testament?

The New Testament is the second part of the Christian Bible. It has 27 books that include the four Gospels, the Acts of the Apostles, the letters of the apostles and the book of Revelation.

These books witness to the life and ministry of Jesus Christ, his teachings, his death and resurrection, and the work of his followers in spreading the message of the gospel.

10 Lessons of the New Testament

Read the Bible references, understand the message of each story and reflect on how to apply its teachings.

LESSON 01

Birth of Jesus Christ

Matthew 1:18–25; Luke 2:1–20

The Gospels describe the birth of Jesus Christ. An angel informed Joseph about the child who would be called Jesus. Luke describes how Jesus was born in Bethlehem and how that news was announced to the shepherds.

Key Lesson:

The birth of Jesus is an important part of the story of God's plan to bring salvation to humanity.

Life Application:

Let us remember the love of God and show love to other people in words and actions.

LESSON 02

Baptism of Jesus

Matthew 3:13–17; Mark 1:9–11

Jesus came to John the Baptist to be baptized in the Jordan River. The Gospel of Matthew describes how the Spirit of God came down upon him and a voice from heaven declared him the beloved Son.

Key Lesson:

The baptism of Jesus shows the beginning of his public ministry and the importance of fulfilling the will of God.

Life Application:

Let us learn humility, obedience and to be ready to serve God with a sincere heart.

LESSON 03

Jesus Calls His Disciples

Matthew 4:18–22; Luke 5:1–11

Jesus called different people, including fishermen, to become his disciples. He taught them and prepared them to share in his ministry.

Key Lesson:

Following Jesus involves learning his teachings and growing in faith and service.

Life Application:

Let us be ready to learn, to help others and to use our gifts for the benefit of people.

LESSON 04

Teachings of Jesus on the Mountain

Matthew 5–7

In the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus taught about humility, mercy, forgiveness, loving enemies, prayer and trusting God.

Key Lesson:

God wants an inner change that is seen in character and in how we treat others.

Life Application:

Let us be faithful, merciful and peace-seeking without taking revenge.

LESSON 05

Miracles of Jesus

Mark 4:35–41; John 6:1–14

The Gospels describe different miracles of Jesus, including calming the storm and feeding a crowd of people. These miracles show his mercy and his authority in the Gospel story.

Key Lesson:

Jesus cared for people who had needs and he taught his disciples to trust God.

Life Application:

Let us care for those in need, work together with others and pray to God in faith.

LESSON 06

Parable of the Good Samaritan

Luke 10:25–37

Jesus told about a man who was injured on the road. Some people passed him by, but a Samaritan stopped, cared for him and made sure he received help. Jesus used this story to teach about a neighbour.

Key Lesson:

True love is seen in acts of mercy, not in words alone.

Life Application:

Let us help people in need without discriminating by tribe, origin or condition.

LESSON 07

Parable of the Prodigal Son

Luke 15:11–32

Jesus told about a son who left home and spent his inheritance. When he returned in repentance, his father welcomed him. This story shows the joy of receiving back the one who was lost.

Key Lesson:

This story shows repentance, mercy, forgiveness and the joy of restoring a relationship.

Life Application:

When we make mistakes, let us admit our faults and seek to restore the relationship. When we can, let us show mercy to others.

LESSON 08

Death of Jesus Christ

Matthew 27; Luke 23; John 19

The Gospels describe that Jesus was crucified. Christians understand his death as an important part of God's plan of salvation and a demonstration of his love for humanity.

Key Lesson:

The death of Jesus shows the seriousness of sin and the love of God emphasized in the Gospels.

Life Application:

Let us value the grace of God, seek forgiveness and show self-sacrificing love for others.

LESSON 09

Resurrection of Jesus

Matthew 28:1–10; Luke 24:1–12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–8

The Gospels describe that after being crucified and buried, Jesus rose again. His followers testified that the tomb was empty and that they met him after the resurrection.

Key Lesson:

The resurrection is the foundation of Christian hope about God's victory over death and the promise of eternal life.

Life Application:

Let us live in hope, remembering that present challenges do not remove the hope taught in the gospel.

LESSON 10

First Church and Apostles

Acts 2:1–47; Acts 4:32–35

The book of Acts describes how the disciples of Jesus received the Holy Spirit and began to preach the gospel. Believers met for teaching, prayer, fellowship and helping one another.

Key Lesson:

The Christian community should be built on teaching, prayer, unity and service.

Life Application:

Let us work together in good things, help those in need and share in learning the Bible.

Salvation through Jesus Christ

The Bible teaches that we are saved by grace, through faith; not by boasting about our works. Good works are part of the new life that God calls us to live.
