FAITH IN EVERYDAY LIFE

Christian Life Today

The Bible is not just a book to read. Its teachings can help us make good decisions, strengthen family, respect others, handle challenges and live with love and faithfulness before God.

How does the Bible relate to our life today?

Christian life involves trusting God and showing our faith in everyday actions. When we face difficult decisions, conflicts, challenges of studies, work, or relationships, we can seek wisdom in the Word of God.

Verse to read: Colossians 3:12–17 and Micah 6:8.

10 Bible Lessons for Life Today

Each lesson has an example of everyday application and a Bible reference you can read more.

Lesson 1

Family and Love

Ephesians 4:2–3; Colossians 3:13–14

A strong family is built through love, respect, patience, good communication, and readiness to forgive.

Application: Listen to family members, speak with respect, and seek a peaceful solution when disagreeing.
Lesson 2

Studies and Hard Work

Proverbs 6:6–8; Colossians 3:23

The Bible encourages us to be hardworking and to use our time and gifts with wisdom. Education requires effort, patience, and responsibility.

Application: Make a study schedule, work faithfully, and ask for help when you fail to understand a lesson.
Lesson 3

Honesty at Work

Proverbs 12:22; Luke 16:10

Honesty means telling the truth, fulfilling our duty, and avoiding deception even when no one is watching us.

Application: Keep promises, respect time, and do not use deception to gain advantage.
Lesson 4

Forgiveness and Peace

Matthew 6:14–15; Romans 12:18

Forgiveness can help heal relationships and reduce pain. Seeking peace does not mean that we should accept being mistreated.

Application: Learn to talk about conflicts calmly. Seek appropriate help if the situation is not safe.
Lesson 5

Helping Others

Galatians 6:2; Matthew 25:35–40

Jesus taught us to show love to people who need help. Service can be seen in small everyday actions.

Application: Help a fellow student, visit someone who needs comfort, or share what you have.
Lesson 6

Technology and Social Media

Philippians 4:8; Ephesians 4:29

Technology can help learning, communication, and work. We need to use it with wisdom and respect others.

Application: Verify information before sharing it, protect your personal information, and avoid messages that humiliate others.
Lesson 7

Money and Responsibility

Proverbs 21:5; 1 Timothy 6:6–10

The Bible warns about loving money too much and encourages us to be faithful in the use of the property we have.

Application: Plan spending, avoid unnecessary spending, and be generous according to your ability.
Lesson 8

Health and Body Care

1 Corinthians 6:19–20; 3 John 1:2

The Bible reminds us to respect our bodies and take care of the life we were given. Health involves good habits and seeking help when needed.

Application: Sleep enough, eat nutritious food, do appropriate physical activities, and seek health advice when needed.
Lesson 9

Prayer and Reading the Bible

Psalm 119:105; 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Prayer helps us talk to God, and reading the Bible helps us learn teachings and reflect on our lives.

Application: Set aside time to read a passage of the Bible, pray, and think about how to apply what you learned.
Lesson 10

Hope and Return of Jesus

John 14:1–3; Titus 2:11–13

Christians have hope in God's promises and the return of Jesus. That hope invites us to live with love, faithfulness, and responsibility.

Application: Continue doing good, treating others with justice, and growing in faith every day.
"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path."

Psalm 119:105

The Word of God can help us choose the path of wisdom in our everyday life.

4 Daily Habits for a Christian

Start with small steps you can continue to do faithfully according to your schedule.

Start with Gratitude

Reflect on things you are grateful for and ask for God's guidance for your day.

Read the Bible

Read a small passage and reflect on its message before continuing with your activities.

Do Good

Find one way to help or encourage another person every day.

Reflect in the Evening

Think about what you learned, thank God for the day, and identify areas you can improve.

Sample Spiritual Schedule

This is a sample you can change according to your schedule of studies, work, family, and worship.

Morning: Pray and read a passage of the Bible.
Noon: Do studies or work with hard work and faithfulness.
During the day: Show love, respect, and help to others.
Evening: Reflect on your day and thank God.

Living by God's Grace

Christian life is not an attempt to earn salvation by our own strength. The Bible teaches that we are saved by God's grace through faith. When we receive His grace, we grow in love and learn to do good works as the fruit of our faith.

Ephesians 2:8–10; Galatians 5:22–23
