What is the New Testament?

The New Testament is the second part of the Christian Bible. It contains 27 books, including the four Gospels, the Acts of the Apostles, the epistles of the apostles, and the Book of Revelation.

These books testify to the life and ministry of Jesus Christ, His teachings, His death and resurrection, and the work of His followers in spreading the message of the gospel.

10 Lessons of the New Testament

Read the Bible references, understand the message of each story, and reflect on how to apply its teachings.

LESSON 01

Birth of Jesus Christ

Matthew 1:18–25; Luke 2:1–20

The Gospels describe the birth of Jesus Christ. An angel informed Joseph about the child who would be named Jesus. Luke explains how Jesus was born in Bethlehem and how this news was announced to the shepherds.

Key lesson:

The birth of Jesus is a vital part of the story of God's plan to bring salvation to humanity.

Life application:

Let us remember God's love and show love to others through both words and actions.

LESSON 02

Baptism of Jesus

Matthew 3:13–17; Mark 1:9–11

Jesus came to John the Baptist to be baptized in the Jordan River. The Gospel of Matthew describes how the Spirit of God descended upon Him and a voice from heaven declared Him to be the beloved Son.

Key lesson:

The baptism of Jesus marks the beginning of His public ministry and the importance of fulfilling God's will.

Life application:

Let us learn humility, obedience, and readiness to serve God with a sincere heart.

LESSON 03

Jesus Calls His Disciples

Matthew 4:18–22; Luke 5:1–11

Jesus called various people, including fishermen, to become His disciples. He taught them and prepared them to participate in His ministry.

Key lesson:

Following Jesus involves learning His teachings and growing in faith and service.

Life application:

Let us be willing to learn, help others, and use our gifts for the benefit of people.

LESSON 04

Teachings of Jesus on the Mount

Matthew 5–7

In the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus taught about humility, mercy, forgiveness, loving one's enemies, prayer, and trusting in God.

Key lesson:

God desires an inner transformation that is reflected in our character and how we treat others.

Life application:

Let us be faithful, merciful, and seekers of peace without seeking revenge.

LESSON 05

Miracles of Jesus

Mark 4:35–41; John 6:1–14

The Gospels recount various miracles of Jesus, including calming a storm and feeding a large crowd. These miracles demonstrate His compassion and authority in the Gospel narrative.

Key lesson:

Jesus cared for people in need and taught His disciples to trust in God.

Life application:

Let us care for those in need, cooperate with others, and pray to God in faith.

LESSON 06

Parable of the Good Samaritan

Luke 10:25–37

Jesus told a story about a man who was injured on the road. Some people passed him by, but a Samaritan stopped for him, cared for him, and ensured he received help. Jesus used this story to teach about who our neighbor is.

Key lesson:

True love is shown through acts of compassion, not in words alone.

Life application:

Let us help people in need without discriminating based on tribe, origin, or status.

LESSON 07

Parable of the Prodigal Son

Luke 15:11–32

Jesus told of a son who left home and squandered his inheritance. When he returned remorseful, his father welcomed him back. This story illustrates the joy of receiving back the one who was lost.

Key lesson:

This story demonstrates repentance, mercy, forgiveness, and the joy of restoring relationships.

Life application:

When we make mistakes, let us acknowledge them and seek to mend relationships. Whenever possible, let us show mercy to others.

LESSON 08

Death of Jesus Christ

Matthew 27; Luke 23; John 19

The Gospels explain that Jesus was crucified. Christians understand His death as a central part of God's plan of salvation and a demonstration of His love for humanity.

Key lesson:

The death of Jesus reveals the seriousness of sin and the love of God emphasized in the Gospels.

Life application:

Let us value God's grace, seek forgiveness, and show sacrificial love for others.

LESSON 09

Resurrection of Jesus

Matthew 28:1–10; Luke 24:1–12; 1 Corinthians 15:3–8

The Gospels state that after being crucified and buried, Jesus rose again. His followers testified that the tomb was empty and that they encountered Him after the resurrection.

Key lesson:

The resurrection is the foundation of Christian hope regarding God's victory over death and the promise of eternal life.

Life application:

Let us live with hope, remembering that present challenges do not take away the hope taught in the gospel.

LESSON 10

The Early Church and Apostles

Acts 2:1–47; Acts 4:32–35

The Book of Acts describes how Jesus' disciples received the Holy Spirit and began preaching the gospel. Believers gathered for teaching, prayer, fellowship, and mutual support.

Key lesson:

The Christian community should be built upon teaching, prayer, unity, and service.

Life application:

Let us cooperate in doing good, help those in need, and participate in studying the Bible together.

Salvation through Jesus Christ

The Bible teaches that we are saved by grace, through faith; not so that we can boast about our works. Good works are part of the new life that God calls us to live.
