Old Testament

Learn how God created the world, how He led His people, how He acted in history and how we can learn from the lives of people mentioned in the Bible.

What does the Old Testament teach us?

The Old Testament has books that explain creation, the beginning of sin, God's covenant with His people, the history of Israel, law, poetry, wisdom and the message of the prophets.

When we read these stories, we should consider their context, what the passage says and the lesson we can get. These lessons help you start; also read the full passages in your Bible.

BIBLE LESSONS

10 Lessons of the Old Testament

Read the story, reflect on its message and see how you can apply its lesson in life.

LESSON 01

Creation of the World

Genesis 1:1–31; Genesis 2:1–3

The Bible begins by explaining that God created heaven and earth. Genesis 1 describes the steps of creation, and Genesis 2:1–3 talks about the seventh day which God sanctified and blessed.

Key Lesson:

Creation reminds us about God as Creator and the duty to care for life and the environment He created.

Life Application:

We should value life, care for the environment and set aside time to rest and worship God.

LESSON 02

Adam, Eve and the Beginning of Sin

Genesis 2:15–25; Genesis 3:1–24

Adam and Eve were placed in the Garden of Eden. Genesis 3 explains how they disobeyed God's commands and how their relationship with God was affected. This story shows the entry of sin into the Bible story.

Key Lesson:

Our decisions have consequences, and we need to depend on God instead of ignoring His commands.

Life Application:

Let us learn to accept our mistakes, seek forgiveness and choose what is good.

LESSON 03

Noah and the Ark

Genesis 6:9–22; Genesis 7–9

In the story of Noah, the world is described as full of wickedness. God instructs Noah to build an ark. After the flood, Genesis 9 describes God's covenant with Noah and the sign of the rainbow.

Key Lesson:

This story emphasizes obedience, the importance of doing what is right and hope in God's promises.

Life Application:

Let us be faithful even when we face difficult situations, and fulfil our duty with hard work.

LESSON 04

Abraham and God's Promise

Genesis 12:1–9; Genesis 15:1–6

God called Abram to leave his country and go to a place He would show him. God promised him that he would become a great nation and that through him nations would be blessed.

Key Lesson:

Abraham shows the importance of faith and trusting God even when we do not know everything about the next step.

Life Application:

Let us have faith, patience and a heart to follow what is right when we make decisions.

LESSON 05

Joseph: Patience and Forgiveness

Genesis 37; Genesis 39–45; Genesis 50:15–21

Joseph was mistreated by his brothers and taken to Egypt. He went through many challenges, but later became a leader with authority. When he met his brothers again, he was able to give them food and talk to them about forgiveness and God's plan.

Key Lesson:

The story of Joseph reminds us about patience, faithfulness and the possibility of forgiving after being mistreated.

Life Application:

Let us not allow hatred to rule our hearts. Let us seek safe and wise ways to resolve conflicts.

LESSON 06

Moses and the Exodus from Egypt

Exodus 3:1–14; Exodus 12–14

Moses was called by God to lead the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt. The book of Exodus describes the struggle to leave Egypt and crossing the Red Sea.

Key Lesson:

This story talks about deliverance, leadership, courage and depending on God in difficult situations.

Life Application:

Let us treat others with justice and be ready to use our ability to help people who are in difficult situations.

LESSON 07

David and Goliath

1 Samuel 17:1–50

Goliath frightened the soldiers of Israel. David, a young shepherd, agreed to face him trusting God's help. The story explains how David defeated Goliath.

Key Lesson:

Courage is not measured by body size. Faith, preparation and using well the ability we have is important.

Life Application:

When we face challenges, let us prepare, seek good advice and not give up quickly.

LESSON 08

Elijah and the True God

1 Kings 18:17–39

Elijah confronted the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel. The story explains how people were invited to decide to follow the true God instead of wavering between two ways.

Key Lesson:

This story encourages faithfulness to God and examining the foundations of our faith.

Life Application:

Let us have a stand in what is good, and examine teachings by comparing them with the Word of God.

LESSON 09

Esther: Courage to Help Others

Esther 4:10–17; Esther 7:1–6

Esther was a queen at a time when the Jews faced danger. Mordecai reminded her that perhaps she had reached her position for such a time as this. Esther took a courageous step to defend her people.

Key Lesson:

Courage can be used to defend justice and help people who are in danger.

Life Application:

Let us speak truth with wisdom, defend the oppressed and use our positions to help others.

LESSON 10

Daniel and the Lions' Den

Daniel 6:1–28

Daniel continued to pray to God even after an order was given against his habit of prayer. He was thrown into the lions' den, but the story explains that God protected him.

Key Lesson:

Daniel shows the importance of faithfulness, spiritual discipline and trusting God in difficult situations.

Life Application:

Let us be faithful in our morals, continue to pray and seek help from people we trust when we face problems.

How to learn each lesson

Follow these steps when you learn the Bible alone or in a group.

1. Read

Open the Bible and read the passage shown carefully. Do not read one sentence alone without understanding its context.

2. Reflect

Ask yourself what the story teaches about God, people, decisions and their consequences.

3. Apply the Lesson

Choose one true and good thing you can apply in your everyday life.
