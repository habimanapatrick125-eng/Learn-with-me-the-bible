WISDOM FROM THE BIBLE

Important Warnings of the Bible

The Bible teaches us to recognize danger, to avoid bad decisions, to repent when we make mistakes, and to choose the way that agrees with love, justice, and the will of God.

Why do we need Bible warnings?

Bible warnings are not meant to teach us fear only. They help us recognize mistakes, understand the consequences of our decisions, and seek a better way to live. When we make mistakes, we can return to God in repentance and seek His help.

Verse to read: Proverbs 3:5–6 and 2 Timothy 3:16–17.

10 Warning Lessons from the Bible

Each lesson has a Bible reference, explanation, and application in everyday life. Read the verses mentioned in your Bible for deeper understanding.

1. Adam and Eve: Consequences of Disobedience

Genesis 2:16–17; 3:1–13

Adam and Eve faced the temptation to disobey God's command. Their story shows the importance of choosing wisely and recognizing the consequences of our decisions.

Warning: Do not follow every advice without examining it according to the Word of God.

2. Cain: Jealousy and Anger

Genesis 4:3–10

Cain became angry because his offering was not received as he wanted. Instead of dealing with his anger, he made a decision that brought him great harm.

Warning: Do not let jealousy and anger rule your decisions. Seek a peaceful way.

3. King Saul: Disobeying God's Commands

1 Samuel 15:10–23

Saul failed to fully follow the commands he had been given. His story reminds us that obedience is important in our relationship with God.

Warning: Do not change God's commands on purpose so they only fit your desires.

4. David: Consequences of Sin

2 Samuel 11:1–17; 12:1–13

David sinned and tried to hide his mistakes. When the prophet Nathan confronted him, David confessed his fault.

Warning: Do not hide your mistakes. Accept the truth, repent, and seek to do what is right.

5. Solomon: Protecting Our Relationship with God

1 Kings 11:1–10

Although Solomon had great wisdom, later he allowed other things to turn him away from his faithfulness to God.

Warning: Wisdom and success do not remove the need to continue seeking God faithfully.

6. Judas Iscariot: Danger of Money and Betrayal

Matthew 26:14–16; 27:3–5

Judas betrayed Jesus for money. His story warns about putting greed for wealth before faithfulness and morals.

Warning: Do not let greed for money make you harm others or leave your principles.

7. Religious Hypocrisy

Matthew 6:1–6; 23:23–28

Jesus warned against showing religious acts only to be praised by people, while ignoring justice, mercy, and faithfulness.

Warning: Do not care only about outward appearance. Build true character, humility, and love.

8. Ananias and Sapphira: Being Unfaithful

Acts 5:1–11

Ananias and Sapphira lied about the money they gave. Their story shows the seriousness of deception and the importance of truth before God.

Warning: Be faithful in your words and actions, even when no one sees you.

9. Rich Fool: Depending on Wealth Alone

Luke 12:13–21

Jesus told about a man who gathered much wealth but did not think enough about his relationship with God.

Warning: Wealth is not a measure of a person's worth. Use your resources with wisdom and generosity.

10. Watchfulness and Being Spiritually Ready

Matthew 24:42–44; 1 Peter 5:8–9

Jesus taught his disciples to watch and be ready. Christians are called to live faithfully, not in spiritual carelessness.

Warning: Do not postpone important spiritual things. Learn the Bible, pray, and do good every day.
"The prudent see danger and take refuge, but the simple keep going and pay the penalty."

Proverbs 22:3

Reflect on how wisdom can help you make good decisions.

How Can We Avoid Mistakes?

These are practical ways that can help us make decisions that agree with Bible teachings.

1. Examine the Word of God

Read the Bible and compare your decisions with its teachings.

Psalm 119:105

2. Pray for Wisdom

Ask God to help you recognize what is good and make decisions wisely.

James 1:5

3. Seek Good Advice

Listen to wise advice from faithful and wise people.

Proverbs 11:14

4. Repent and Learn

When you make a mistake, accept the fault, ask for forgiveness, and take steps to correct what you can.

1 John 1:9

God Gives Forgiveness and Hope

Bible warnings invite us to change, but they do not mean that a person who made a mistake has no hope. When we return to God in true repentance, we receive forgiveness and help to start walking in a better way. Salvation is by God's grace through faith; good works are the result of that faith.

Ephesians 2:8–10; 1 John 1:9
