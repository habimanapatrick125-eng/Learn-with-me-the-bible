KNOWING JESUS CHRIST

Life and Teachings of Jesus

Learn about the life of Jesus Christ, his love for humanity, his teachings, miracles, death and resurrection. Discover how his example can guide our lives.

Why learn about Jesus?

Jesus Christ is the centre of the Gospel message. In the Bible, we learn about his love, his mercy, his teachings, and his sacrifice for the salvation of humanity.

When we read the stories of Jesus, we get guidance on how to love God, to love our neighbours, to forgive, to serve others, and to live by faith.

Verse to read: John 14:6 and John 3:16.

10 Lessons About the Life of Jesus

Each lesson has a Bible reference and a lesson to apply in everyday life. Read the passages mentioned to understand the full story.

Lesson 1

Birth of Jesus

Matthew 1:18–25; Luke 2:1–20

Jesus was born in Bethlehem. His birth fulfilled God's promise and brought a message of hope to humanity.

We learn: God can fulfil His purposes through ordinary situations and humble people.
Lesson 2

Baptism of Jesus

Matthew 3:13–17

Jesus was baptized by John the Baptist in the Jordan River. That event showed the beginning of his public ministry.

We learn: Humility, obedience, and the importance of doing the will of God.
Lesson 3

Calling the Disciples

Matthew 4:18–22; Luke 5:1–11

Jesus called different people to follow him and learn from him. His disciples shared in the ministry of spreading the Gospel.

We learn: To learn from Jesus and to use our gifts to help others.
Lesson 4

Sermon on the Mount

Matthew 5–7

Jesus taught about humility, mercy, forgiveness, love for enemies, and the importance of doing what is right.

We learn: Faith should be seen in character, words, and actions.
Lesson 5

Miracles of Jesus

Mark 4:35–41; John 6:1–14

The Bible tells how Jesus calmed the storm and fed a large crowd of people. His miracles revealed his authority and his mercy for people.

We learn: To turn to God in our needs and to be ready to help others.
Lesson 6

Example of the Good Samaritan

Luke 10:25–37

Jesus told about a Samaritan who helped an injured man while others passed by without helping him.

We learn: Our neighbour is a person we should show love to in actions, without discrimination.
Lesson 7

Jesus and Forgiveness

Luke 23:32–34; Matthew 18:21–22

Jesus taught about the importance of forgiving. He showed love even when he was tortured.

We learn: To let go of revenge and seek peace, while using wisdom and appropriate boundaries.
Lesson 8

Death of Jesus and Its Meaning

Matthew 27:32–54; John 19:16–30

Jesus died on the cross. Christians believe that his sacrifice is a central part of God's plan to redeem humanity from sin.

We learn: Self-sacrificing love, the grace of God, and the importance of seeking reconciliation with Him.
Lesson 9

Resurrection of Jesus

Matthew 28:1–10; Luke 24:1–12

The Bible says that Jesus rose from the dead. The resurrection is an important foundation of Christian hope and the promise of eternal life.

We learn: To live in hope and to trust God even when we face hard times.
Lesson 10

Promise of the Return of Jesus

John 14:1–3; Acts 1:9–11

Jesus told his disciples about the hope of his return. Christians are called to live faithfully and to be spiritually ready.

We learn: To remain in faith, love, service, and hope while waiting for God's promises.
"I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."

John 14:6

How Can We Follow the Example of Jesus?

Following Jesus involves learning from him and showing his character in our relationships with others.

Love Others

Show people love, mercy, and respect.

John 13:34–35

Pray to God

Set aside time to pray and seek God.

Mark 1:35

Serve Others

Use your time and gifts to help people.

Mark 10:43–45

Follow His Teachings

Read the Bible and apply its teachings with wisdom.

John 14:15

Jesus and Our Salvation

The Bible teaches that salvation is a gift of God's grace received through faith. We cannot buy salvation by our works. Instead, when we believe in Christ, our lives should show the fruit of faith through love, obedience, and good works.

Ephesians 2:8–10; Romans 5:8
