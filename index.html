Why should we learn the Bible?

The Bible helps us to know God, to understand His love and to learn how to live by faith.

Knowing God

Through the Bible, we learn about the character of God, His love, His creation and His plan for humanity.

Jeremiah 9:23–24

Following Jesus

We learn the teachings of Jesus Christ, His example of love, forgiveness, humility and service to others.

John 14:6

Living by Faith

The Bible teaches us to have hope, to make wise decisions, to love one another and to trust God in challenges.

Proverbs 3:5–6

FAITH AND TEACHINGS

Seventh-day Adventist Church

The Seventh-day Adventist Church is a Christian community that emphasizes the importance of the Bible, believing in Jesus Christ, living a spiritual life and looking forward to His return.

Seventh-day Adventists observe the Sabbath as a holy day of rest and worship, from Friday evening to Saturday evening, according to their teachings from the Bible.

Important things we learn
  • The Bible as the foundation of faith.
  • Jesus Christ as our Saviour.
  • The importance of the Sabbath and worship.
  • Love for God and neighbour.
  • Hope of the return of Jesus Christ.

Exodus 20:8–11; John 14:1–3; Revelation 14:12
