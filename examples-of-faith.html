Examples of Faith in the Bible

Discover the lives of people who believed in God, endured trials, and learned to depend on Him in different situations. Their examples can help us grow in our faith today.

What is faith?

Faith is trusting God and relying on His promises, even when we do not understand everything that is happening. True faith is seen in our relationship with God, our obedience, our love for others, and our patience when we face challenges.

Verse to read: Hebrews 11:1.

10 People in the Bible Who Teach Us Faith

Read their stories, open the Bible verses, and think about how you can apply these lessons in your life.

Lesson 1

Abraham: Faith and Obedience

Genesis 12:1–4; 15:1–6

God called Abraham to leave his country and go to a place He would show him. Abraham believed God's promise and began that journey.

We learn: To trust God and follow His guidance, even when we do not know every step of the journey.
Lesson 2

Moses: Depending on God

Exodus 3:1–12; 14:10–31

God called Moses to lead the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt. Moses faced fear and challenges, but God guided him in that work.

We learn: God can help us fulfil our duty even when we feel we are not capable enough.
Lesson 3

Ruth: Faithfulness and Love

Ruth 1:14–18; 2:11–12

Ruth chose to stay with Naomi and help her in a difficult time. Her life shows faithfulness, love, and devotion.

We learn: To be faithful to the people we love and to help those going through hard times.
Lesson 4

Esther: Courage and Duty

Esther 4:13–16; 7:1–6

Esther faced a difficult decision to speak for her people. She showed courage when she agreed to take important action.

We learn: To use the opportunities we have to help others and to stand for what is good.
Lesson 5

Daniel: Faithfulness to God

Daniel 1:8; 6:10–23

Daniel continued to be faithful to God even when he faced pressure. He continued to pray and hold on to his faith.

We learn: To hold on to good principles and continue to seek God in our lives.
Lesson 6

David: Depending on God in Challenges

1 Samuel 17:32–50

David faced Goliath depending on God instead of depending on his own size or strength.

We learn: To face problems with courage, wisdom, preparation, and depending on God.
Lesson 7

Job: Faith in Trials

Job 1:20–22; 2:9–10; 19:25

Job went through suffering and lost important things in his life. His story invites us to think about faith and hope in times of pain.

We learn: We can tell God our sorrows and seek help without giving up hope.
Lesson 8

Joseph: Patience and Forgiveness

Genesis 39:1–23; 45:1–15; 50:20

Joseph went through betrayal and prison, but he continued to be faithful. Later he chose to forgive his brothers and help them.

We learn: To continue doing good in difficult situations and to seek a way to forgive others.
Lesson 9

Mary: Accepting God's Will

Luke 1:26–38; 1:46–49

Mary received the angel's message about the birth of Jesus and answered with humility, showing readiness to obey God.

We learn: To approach God with humility and to be ready to follow His guidance.
Lesson 10

Paul: Faith and Patience in Ministry

Acts 9:1–22; 2 Timothy 4:7–8

After meeting Jesus, Paul changed the direction of his life and devoted himself to preaching the Gospel. He endured many challenges in his ministry.

We learn: We can change, grow spiritually, and use our gifts to help others.

How Can We Grow Our Faith?

Faith grows when we build our relationship with God and apply the teachings of the Bible in everyday life.

Read the Bible

Set aside time to read the Word of God and reflect on its meaning.

Pray to God

Talk to God about your gratitude, your needs, and your challenges.

Love Others

Show love through kindness, forgiveness, faithfulness, and service.

Fellowship with Believers

Learn together with others and seek spiritual advice built on the Bible.

Faith and Salvation

The Bible teaches that we are saved by the grace of God through faith, not by earning salvation by our works. Good works are the result of our faith and our love for God and for our neighbours.

Ephesians 2:8–10
